Understanding what disqualifies your purchases from cashback is equally important as understanding how to earn it. TopCashback has specific terms and conditions, and violating them means forfeiting your earnings entirely.

The solution is simple: only use coupon codes explicitly listed on TopCashback's Estée Lauder offer page or on Estée Lauder's official site.

**Mistake #2: Purchasing Gift Cards Through Your TopCashback Link**

Gift card purchases don't qualify for cashback—this is explicit in TopCashback's terms. If you click through TopCashback and purchase Estée Lauder gift cards, you forfeit the cashback. However, buying actual Estée Lauder products does earn cashback, and then you can redeem your earned cashback as gift cards later at a bonus rate.

**Mistake #3: Enabling Ad Blockers or VPN Services**

Some browser plugins and tools interfere with TopCashback's tracking capability. Ad blockers like AdBlock, uBlock, and Brave browser's native tracking prevention can prevent TopCashback from tracking your purchase properly, resulting in zero cashback despite a legitimate transaction.

Using VPN services, DNS-level ad blocking, or other privacy tools also sometimes blocks the tracking cookies TopCashback relies on. The solution is disabling these tools temporarily while making TopCashback purchases or ensuring they're configured to allow TopCashback's tracking scripts.

**Mistake #4: Switching Between Multiple Cashback Portals for a Single Purchase**

If you click through TopCashback to Estée Lauder, add items to your cart, then leave and return through Rakuten, your cashback will attach to whichever portal you came through most recently. This overwrites your TopCashback tracking, and if Rakuten has lower cashback rates that day, you've essentially downgraded your earnings.

The solution is intentional: pick one portal based on their current rate, complete your transaction, and don't hop between portals mid-purchase.

**Mistake #5: Making Purchases Through Your Mobile App**

TopCashback's tracking works through the web browser system, not through retailer mobile apps. If you click through TopCashback but then open Estée Lauder's mobile app to complete your purchase, the tracking disconnects and your cashback fails to register.

Always complete your entire purchase through the web browser after clicking through TopCashback's link to ensure tracking is maintained throughout the transaction.

**Mistake #6: Filing Disputed Claims Too Late**

TopCashback permits cashback claims within 100 days of the purchase date. If your purchase doesn't appear as expected and you wait longer than 100 days to submit a claim, TopCashback cannot accept it regardless of the circumstances.

The solution is taking a screenshot of your TopCashback cashback offer and your order confirmation immediately after purchase—then keeping these screenshots for 100 days. If your cashback doesn't appear as expected, you have documented evidence and ample time to file a claim.
